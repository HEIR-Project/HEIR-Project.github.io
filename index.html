01 / The missing perspective

“Bring that over.”
What does that mean?

A request is more than a sentence. Its meaning depends on a person's attention, activity, and the interactions that came before it.

HEIR preserves both sides of the interaction: human egocentric video, measured gaze, and speech alongside robot observations and teleoperated navigation–manipulation trajectories.

WHAT DOES THE HUMAN MEAN?

Ground the request.

Use speech, measured gaze, human and robot views, and interaction history to infer a grounded task description, target objects, destinations, and a nominal execution plan.

Metrics: Slot F1 · Location accuracy · Plan exact match · Entity–visibility F1
